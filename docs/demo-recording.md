# FoodLoop — Short demo recording guide

## Before recording

1. Open the FoodLoop Preview from the project dashboard and wait for its live landing page (not the building placeholder).
2. Click **Choose a demo page**. The public `/explore` screen picker contains ten working routes; each entry opens the correct sample role. Demo data stays in this browser and makes no shared database writes.
3. Keep the logo and status labels visible, record at desktop width, then narrow the viewport to show the picker and workspace at phone width.
4. Present this as a product walkthrough—not a claim that live couriers, maps, messages or verified food-safety decisions exist.

## Suggested 75–100 second sequence

1. **0:00–0:12 — Why FoodLoop.** Show the landing page's warm, 3D-inspired food artwork and three-step loop. Use **Choose a demo page** to open the separate screen picker, then point out the ten selectable features.
2. **0:12–0:28 — Donor listing.** Choose **List surplus food** (donor role). Show the food name, category, quantity, servings, preparation, pickup, storage, allergens and donor-supplied safety-note fields, plus the use-by/ETA warning. Optionally choose a small image to show the local-only preview. Create the sample listing.
3. **0:28–0:45 — NGO review.** Use the demo role menu to switch to **NGO / food bank**. Open the new listing, review its safety, allergen, capacity and time details, then accept it. Point out that recommendations are constrained by the use-by deadline.
4. **0:45–1:00 — Delivery.** Switch to **Delivery partner**. Accept the matched route from **Delivery jobs**, open its tracking page, confirm pickup and confirm the final handoff. Show the activity timeline and delivered quantity.
5. **1:00–1:20 — Resilience.** Return to sample listing `FL-2418` or the overview, choose the donor/NGO/admin demo role and select **Simulate delivery failure**. Open the donation detail to show prior assignment history and the suggested backup. Confirm the reassignment as an authorized non-partner role.
6. **1:20–1:35 — Close.** Show the estimated impact screen and floating Loop support widget. State that metrics, tracking, map routes, sample identities, messages and demo confirmations are illustrative/simulated; no impact figure is audited.

## Reset and alternate capture

Use **Profile → Reset demo data** to restore the deterministic sample records and restart the walkthrough. If capture time is short, enter through the landing page, select **Overview dashboard**, then use `FL-2418` for the pre-seeded backup-partner exception.

## Editing / submission notes

Keep the raw capture and any screenshots outside Git unless the organizers request them. Confirm repository URL/visibility, the final deadline and time zone, and the submission form/format directly with the hackathon organizers. Do not add invented teammate credits or claim real integrations that are not configured.
